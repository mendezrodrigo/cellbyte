## Cellbyte — Founder’s Associate Case Project

This repository documents a **48-hour Founder’s Associate interview case** completed for Cellbyte.

As part of the process, I analyzed Cellbyte’s positioning, digital presence, discoverability, LinkedIn activity, and visual communication, then translated those findings into practical deliverables.

One of the final outputs was a **recreated and redesigned Cellbyte landing page**, developed as a static frontend prototype to demonstrate how the company’s positioning, brand system, hierarchy, and messaging could be expressed more clearly and professionally online.

The project combined:

* startup research and competitive analysis,
* discoverability testing across search and AI platforms,
* LinkedIn content and growth strategy,
* brand and UX interpretation,
* landing-page redesign,
* HTML/CSS frontend implementation,
* and Founder’s Associate-style synthesis under a short deadline.

The repository therefore serves both as a **case-study artifact** and as a record of how strategic analysis can be translated directly into an improved product and brand surface.

> **48 hours from research to strategy to implementation.**
